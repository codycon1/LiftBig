LIFTBIG

Privacy policy · Effective October 9, 2026

LiftBig is a workout journal. This page explains what the iPhone app and the website do with your information. LiftBig is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Data stored on your device

Workouts, plans, settings, themes, and favorites are saved on the device where you use LiftBig. The website uses that browser’s local storage. The iPhone app saves the same information in the app’s private storage. Deleting the app, or clearing the website’s site data, removes that copy.

You can export a backup file and import it later, including from the website into the iPhone app. LiftBig does not operate an account system and does not run its own database of your journal.

iCloud

The iPhone app can optionally sync that journal through your private iCloud account. The toggle is off until you turn it on. Apple stores that data with your Apple ID. LiftBig cannot see it. Turning the toggle off leaves the copy already on that iPhone and stops further syncing.

Apple Health

If you allow it, LiftBig reads your body weight and height from Apple Health and writes workout sessions you log (activity type, duration, distance when you entered one, and an energy estimate). LiftBig does not read sleep, heart rate, or clinical records. You can turn this off in Settings. Turning it off does not delete workouts already saved in Apple Health.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS shows a notification at the time you chose. The rest timer can also notify you when rest ends, including on the Lock Screen. LiftBig does not control Apple’s Clock app.

Purchases

The app is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. Later, an optional LiftBig Pro subscription may unlock companion features such as Apple Watch logging and adaptive workout suggestions. The training journal and its free updates stay available to everyone who bought the app. LiftBig does not use the purchase to build an advertising profile.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or use third-party tracking.
  • We do not require a LiftBig account.

Children

LiftBig is not directed at children under 13.
